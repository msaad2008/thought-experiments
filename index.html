    Why these five?

    Each game is built on one idea that broke classical physics, the rules that worked for centuries until experiments proved them wrong. Moving clocks really do tick slower, and gravity is really curved space. Two particles can stay linked across any distance, a particle's position and speed can't both be pinned down, and smashing particles together turns energy into new ones. You won't learn these ideas by reading about them; you'll learn them by playing until they click.
